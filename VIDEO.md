# Project demo video

A screen recording of the Study Tracker running locally, walking through registration,
adding a course, logging study sessions, creating a goal and the dashboard charts.

**Video link:** <REPLACE WITH YOUR YOUTUBE / UNLISTED VIDEO URL>

## What the recording shows

1. `npm run seed` and both servers starting
2. Registering a new account and being redirected to the dashboard
3. Adding a course on the Courses page, editing it, and the validation error on a duplicate code
4. Logging a study session and seeing the dashboard totals and streak update
5. Creating a goal and watching its progress bar fill from the logged sessions
6. Changing the weekly target on the Profile page and the weekly progress card reacting
7. Logging out and being redirected to the login page by the protected route

## How the recording was made

OBS Studio, display capture, 1080p. Sound and narration are optional per the course instructions.
